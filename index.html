THE LONG SECTION

The engineering inside things you never see

A long section is the drawing engineers use to cut along a pipeline, road or tunnel and reveal everything hidden below the surface. The Long Section is a YouTube channel that does the same for water plants, tunnels, foundations and the machines that changed engineering, explained with animated diagrams and sourced facts.

Narration and visuals are produced with AI tools; topic, research and editorial direction by NOVA DIGITAL.
