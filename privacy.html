Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers "The Long Section Uploader", a private publishing tool operated by NOVA DIGITAL for its own YouTube channel, The Long Section. The tool is used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to the public and does not let other people sign in.

What Google data the tool uses

  • With the channel owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, thumbnails and captions to The Long Section, set their titles, descriptions and publishing times, and add them to playlists.
  • It reads basic information about the channel's own videos (for example upload status) to confirm that publishing worked.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or process personal data of viewers or any other YouTube users.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any Google user data with anyone.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling or training AI models.

Storage and security

The access credential for the channel is stored only as an encrypted secret in the tool's private hosting environment. It is never published or shared. The channel owner can withdraw access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Compliance

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact details on The Long Section's YouTube channel page.
